# Chronicles of the Valley

A small action RPG made with the Tiny Swords asset pack. Pick a hero, survive three waves of invaders and defeat the Flame Warden.

Play it online: <https://valleygame.netlify.app/>

## Run locally

```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then open <http://localhost:8000>.

## Controls

- `1`–`4` or click: choose a hero / upgrade
- `WASD` or arrow keys: move
- `Space` or click: attack
- `P` or `Esc`: pause
- `M`: toggle sound
- `Enter`: fight again, `R`: back to hero selection
- `F3`: show FPS (or open the page with `?fps`)

On touch screens, drag on the left side to move and tap the right side to attack.

## Heroes

- **Warrior:** balanced melee fighter
- **Archer:** fast ranged attacker
- **Lancer:** durable, with extra reach
- **Monk:** area pulse that damages enemies and heals
